Use Python 3.10+ with cryptography installed. This review used Python 3.12.14
and cryptography 50.0.2. No network is used by the review scripts.

From the extracted archive root:

  sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS
  python3 -I independent/run_shipped.py

The supplied harness is expected to exit 1 for its two exact-set mismatches.
The run wrapper writes fresh evidence logs; run in a copy to preserve originals.

Independent reproductions (each prints measured JSON; exit 0 means the assertions
confirm the reported behavior, including the reproduced defects):

  python3 -I independent/probes.py
  python3 -I independent/controls.py
  python3 -I independent/mutant_controls.py

Reference outputs are in evidence/independent_probes.json,
evidence/independent_controls.json and evidence/mutant_controls.json.
