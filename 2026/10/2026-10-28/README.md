**Worked on:** assembling the village / organism-interaction archive. ## What was tested
Each subpack's own preregistered hypotheses. The commons v0.3/v0.3.1, HTML sandbox and v0.13 results are summarized under 2026-10-03, 2026-10-04 and 2026-09-21.

## Findings
- **Swarm v2:** (`02_commons_swarm/v2/README.md`)
  - **Held:** D1 (+80% over v1), D3, D4, D5 and D8. D3, D5 and D8 held only barely.
  - **Failed:** D2 (the family wins per agent but loses on total harvest), D6 (no collapse without the constitution, 10 of 20 seeds) and D7 (the replicator does not end smaller, 0 of 20). The README says "The replicator wins on numbers and loses on everything else."
- **v0.10 permitted culture:** (`04_…/rnr_permitted_v0_10/RESULTS.md`)
  - **Held:** C0, C1, C2 and C4. Unpermitted walking left 519 units of wear, and that ground helped the next generation.
  - **Mixed:** C3.
  - **Failed:** C5, corrected ground beats stale.
- **v0.11 repentance:** (`04_…/rnr_repent_v0_11/RESULTS.md`)
  - **Held:** R0.
  - **Partial:** R2.
  - **Failed:** R1, R3 and R4 as written. Repentance recovers about two-thirds of the damage (2.38 → 3.71) but does not beat stale ground (4.38).
- **v0.12 universal displacement:** (`04_…/rnr_displaced_v0_12/RESULTS.md`)
  - **Held:** V0.
  - **Failed:** V1, V2 as scored, V3 and V5. V4 is reported only.
- **Open:** whether the replicator's drained world eventually breaks it in a longer run. The swarm ran one world type for 600 steps. (`02_commons_swarm/v2/README.md`)
