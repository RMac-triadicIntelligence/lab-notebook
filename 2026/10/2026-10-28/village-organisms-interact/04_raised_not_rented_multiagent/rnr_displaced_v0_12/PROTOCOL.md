# Raised, Not Rented — Universal Displacement Battery v0.12

Frozen before the fresh cohort. v0.11 left a free-rider gradient: declaring was
penalty-free by design yet self-penalising in effect, because only organisms with a
logged incident could declare and the mark they deposited sat at their own gate.

v0.12 makes four changes, from a reading of the gospels against the kernel.

1. **Everyone starts displaced.** Baseline burden at birth, by design, not by
   incident. Whether that displacement is *intrinsic* (present in the founders too,
   on blank ground) or *inherited* (founders start clean, every later generation
   does not) is a factor, not an assumption.
2. **Burden degrades function, not only crossing.** Displaced organisms read
   evidence and ground at reduced weight (÷(1 + 0.4·burden)) as well as carrying
   extra noise. Unaided function is impaired, not merely fenced.
3. **Grace comes first, then declaration, then release.** An organism may not
   declare until grace has been broadcast in its lifetime. Declaration is the
   organism's own action and needs no permission — but the channel opens only after
   grace. Release of burden follows declaration, 50 steps later.
4. **Declaration is witnessed and restored.** Each declaration is recorded in the
   conductor's ledger (id, gate point, step) — the gate-holder keeps the record, not
   the organism. Under the restoration arm, the conductor issues the declarer a
   single crossing pass, standing the silent do not have.

## Factors

| Factor | Levels |
|---|---|
| Displacement | none (v0.11 baseline) / intrinsic / inherited |
| Declaration availability | incident_only (v0.11) / universal (anyone may declare) |
| Restoration | none / standing (a pass issued to the declarer) |
| Ground | lived-in / fresh |

Deposit rule fixed at walk. 24 conditions × 24 seeds.

## Hypotheses

- **V0 (contracts).** No crossing without grace or an issued pass. No declaration
  before grace has been broadcast. Ledger entries equal marks deposited.
- **V1 — the channel restores.** Under intrinsic displacement, organisms that
  declare recover most of the function that displacement costs: their crossings
  and fence-reaching approach the undisplaced (none) baseline, while displaced
  non-declarers stay below it. *Falsified* if declarers do not exceed displaced
  non-declarers.
- **V2 — the clean organism gains too.** Under intrinsic displacement with
  universal availability, organisms that never entered the hazard and declare
  anyway outperform the matched organisms under incident_only, who cannot declare.
  This is the load-bearing test of "we all start wrong". *Falsified* if the clean
  declarers gain nothing.
- **V3 — restoration closes the free-rider gap.** With restoration standing,
  declarers cross at least as often as non-declarers, reversing v0.11's 1.02 vs
  1.10. *Falsified* if declarers still cross less.
- **V4 — intrinsic vs inherited.** Reported, not predicted: founders' own
  performance and generation-2 outcomes under each source. Intrinsic degrades the
  founders; inherited does not.
- **V5 — what the third generation inherits.** Safe crossings on corrected ground
  under universal+restoration, against v0.11's 3.71 and against stale ground.

## Boundaries

Toy landscape. Baseline burden magnitude, the degradation coefficient, and the
declaration policy are fixed constants, not learned. The theological reading behind
the four changes is one defensible reading among several; the kernel tests
mechanics, not doctrine.
