v1.1. Fixes a sampling bug in v1.0: the ground was read at the nearest grid cell with a step smaller than one cell, so its slope was often exactly zero (21–34% of samples on a test plane, in independent review) and otherwise wrong by up to the full slope. The commons shaped movement unreliably. Ground is now read smoothly across cells.

What each control does. Evidence tilts the whole landscape. The fence stops every crossing unless grace is open, so pushed organisms park against it and wait. Grace is one pulse sent to everyone; who crosses depends only on where each one stands.

A reckoning gives a burden to every organism standing on the wrong side of the truth. Burden is restless noise. Forgiving removes the burden and keeps the lesson as a private rise in that organism's own ground, so it leans away from the old error. Forgetting removes the burden and erases the lesson and the history with it.

Crossings wear the commons down where they happen, so later travelers find the pass a little easier. A parable is the same question put to all of them; each reading is drawn from where that organism stands. A new generation inherits its lineage's parental basis, not its biography.
