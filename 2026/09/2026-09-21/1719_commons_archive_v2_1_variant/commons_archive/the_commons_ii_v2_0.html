  1. Still living.

What this asks. The founders wear a pass into shared ground. Then they retire. Their biographies are kept here for you, and withheld from everyone who comes next. Three copies of the same arrival then run side by side: one on the ground the founders actually left, one on blank ground, one on ground carrying the same amount of wear moved somewhere else. Same newcomers, same evidence, same random draws. Only the ground differs.

Then the season turns. The worn pass silts up, and the east side beyond it goes bad. Inheritance that only makes movement easier will march the newcomers straight into it. Useful inheritance gets them to a gate sooner and still lets them stop, dwell, and find another way — and wear a better pass for whoever comes third.

Crossing still requires grace in every world. Terrain changes where an organism stands and how ready it is. It never opens the gate.
